    About the company

    Societe Nationale des Telecommunications du Senegal SA (SONATEL) is a Senegal-based company engaged in the telecommunications services industry. The Company offers services covering fixed and mobile communications, voice and short message services, data transmission, the Internet, Ethernet, IP solutions, as well as multimedia and cell phones. Its products and services are targeted at individual customers, businesses and other telecommunications and operators.
